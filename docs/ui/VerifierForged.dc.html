Template explanation

Checks 1 to 3 only show that the proof agrees with itself. Anyone who edits a document can rebuild those hashes.

Check 4b compares against the root on the blockchain. Only the registry's publisher key can write it, once, and it cannot be overwritten.

To check the copy you were sent, fetch the original through its receipt QR and compare the two.
